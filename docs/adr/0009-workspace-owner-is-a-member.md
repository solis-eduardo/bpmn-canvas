# The Workspace Owner is stored as a Member, and membership has no status

Status: accepted

Issue #26 is the first Workspace ticket to actually persist Members, and it forced two small but hard-to-reverse modeling calls, since several later tickets (putting a Diagram into a Workspace, transferring Workspace ownership, mirroring Members from a Keycloak group) build directly on top of `workspace_members`.

**The Workspace Owner gets a `workspace_members` row like any other Member, instead of being excluded from the table the way a Diagram Owner is excluded from `diagram_collaborators` (ADR-0006).** The two situations look similar but aren't: a Diagram's Collaborator is explicitly defined as someone with access "they're not the Diagram Owner of" (CONTEXT.md, "Collaborator") — the two are disjoint by definition. A Workspace's Member has no such exclusion; CONTEXT.md's "Workspace" entry says the creator "becomes its Workspace Owner" without saying they stop being a Member, and issue #26's acceptance criteria says so explicitly ("becomes its Workspace Owner, and a Member"). Giving the Owner a real membership row means `Workspace::members()`/`hasMember()` alone answer "who belongs here" — the question every future access check (editing a Workspace's Diagrams, seeing "my Workspaces") will ask — without every call site having to remember to OR in the Owner separately. We rejected mirroring the Diagram convention (Owner tracked only via `owner_id`, membership checks doing `isOwnedBy() || hasMember()` everywhere) because that duplicates the same OR at every future call site for no benefit: nothing in this domain needs "Members excluding the Owner" as a first-class query.

**`workspace_members` has no status column (no "invited"/"pending" state, unlike `diagram_collaborators`'s `approved`/`requested`).** A Diagram's Collaborator can arrive via two routes that need to be told apart (direct invite vs. self-request awaiting approval — ADR context in `DiagramCollaborator`). CONTEXT.md's "Member" entry describes only one route into a Workspace today: "Members are added by the Workspace Owner" (manual) or, later, mirrored from a Keycloak group — there's no self-request flow for Workspaces in this issue or its dependents. Adding a status column now, with only one real value, would be speculative. If a future ticket introduces a request/approval flow for Workspaces, it can add the column then; nothing here blocks that.

## Consequences

- `Workspace::hasMember($user)` is the single source of truth for "does this User see this Workspace, and can they edit its Diagrams" — it returns `true` for the Owner too, with no special-casing.
- Removing a Member is a hard delete of the row (mirroring `DiagramCollaborator::destroy`'s "declines/removes ⇒ deleted", not a status change).
- The Workspace Owner's row can't be deleted through the "remove a Member" action (`WorkspaceMemberController::destroy` rejects it) — per ADR-0006, the Owner can't leave without transferring ownership first, and that transfer is a separate, not-yet-implemented ticket.
- `owner_id` still lives on `workspaces` (mirroring `diagrams.owner_id`) rather than a role flag inside `workspace_members`, because "who manages this Workspace" needs to stay a single cheap lookup (`isOwnedBy()`), and a future ownership transfer only has to update one column, not migrate a flag between two rows.
- Workspace names aren't required to be unique. Nothing in CONTEXT.md or issue #26 asks for it, and Diagram titles (the closest precedent) aren't unique either — a duplicate-name Workspace is disambiguated by its Members/Owner, the same way a duplicate-title Diagram is disambiguated by its Slug.
